# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Effective: January 2026)

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for: business travel (flights, trains, taxis), client meals (up to $75 per person), accommodation for business trips, and approved software/tools under $200 without prior manager approval.

## Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted via the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred, with an itemized receipt attached. Claims submitted after 30 days require VP-level approval.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (except at approved client dinners, up to $40 per person), personal entertainment, and first-class flight upgrades are not reimbursable without prior written approval from a department head.

## International Travel
Employees traveling internationally for business receive a per diem of $85/day for meals and incidentals, in addition to accommodation and transport reimbursement.
