# IT Security & Data Handling Policy (Effective: January 2026)

## Password Requirements
All employees must use passwords of at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.

## Device Policy
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and endpoint protection software installed before connecting to internal systems. Personal devices may only access email via the approved MDM (mobile device management) application.

## Data Classification
- **Public**: Marketing materials, published blog posts
- **Internal**: Meeting notes, internal wikis
- **Confidential**: Financial reports, employee records, client contracts
- **Restricted**: Salary data, medical/health information, social security numbers, performance review scores

Confidential and Restricted data must never be shared via personal email, unencrypted chat, or copied to personal devices.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected data breach or lost device must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery via security@company.internal or the emergency hotline.

## Access Review
Access to Restricted data (e.g., payroll systems, HR records databases) is reviewed quarterly and requires manager + department head sign-off.
