# Leave Policy (Effective: March 2023) [DEPRECATED — superseded by Leave Policy 2026]

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Up to 3 unused days may carry over to the following year.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member, and 1 day for extended family members.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance.
