# Leave Policy (Effective: January 2026)

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 2 days per month.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the following year.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and 2 days for extended family members.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance, except in cases of emergency or bereavement, which may be submitted retroactively within 10 business days.
